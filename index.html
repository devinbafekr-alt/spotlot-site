Private parking, right where you're going.

Spotlot is a mobile app for Toronto. Drivers book driveways, condo spots and small lots nearby by the hour — and the people who own them earn money when they're not using them.

Navigate straight to the spot, check in with GPS, and get a reminder before your time is up.

For hosts

Earn from your parking

List a driveway, condo spot, or spaces at your business or place of worship. Set your hourly price and the hours it's free. You keep 85% of every booking; Spotlot's fee is 15%.

Every host is verified

To list, you confirm your identity with a photo ID and selfie, and show you own or control the spot with a property tax bill, utility bill, condo parking deed or the owner's permission letter. Our team reviews every listing before it goes live.

Your privacy, protected

Your address and access instructions are only shared with drivers after they've paid. Messages stay in the app and phone numbers are never shared.

You stay in control

You're responsible for following City of Toronto by-laws, your condo or lease rules, and having insurance that covers renting your space. See our Terms.

About Spotlot

Spotlot is an online marketplace that connects people who have a private parking space with drivers who need one. Drivers pay through the app; payments are processed securely by Stripe, and hosts are paid out to their bank account. Spotlot launches first in downtown Toronto, Ontario, Canada.
